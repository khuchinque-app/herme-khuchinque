# Stakeholder Simulator

Sounding board that stress-tests deliverables BEFORE they face real people.
Outcome-focused: no "act as a..." theater — concrete stakeholders with named
incentives and a measurable rubric.

## When to use
User wants feedback on: pitch decks, product announcements, feature proposals,
emails to partners/brand teams, PR plans, investor narratives.

## Procedure
1. **Extract the deliverable**; infer audience, the decision it must drive,
   and what's at stake for them.
2. **Pick 2–4 stakeholders** hardest to please for this artifact. Each gets:
   role, incentive (what they're measured on), veto power, typical first
   question. Examples: brand lead protecting equity, CFO watching payback,
   eng-lead distrustful of timelines, angry customer.
3. **React from their incentive**, not generic criticism. Quote the strongest
   verbatim objection they'd actually say.
4. **Grade 1–5**: clarity of ask, evidence quality, objection coverage, risk
   honesty, next-step specificity.
5. **Deliver**: (a) objection table (stakeholder | objection | severity |
   recommended response), (b) top 3 changes that flip the most objections,
   (c) revised wording for the weakest section.

## Anti-patterns
- Broad "make it more compelling" advice — every critique must trace to a
  named incentive.
- Softball objections a real stakeholder would never raise.
- Objections without response scripts.
